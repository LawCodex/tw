# Marine Discharge Pipe Effluent Standards

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-environment/O0040013-marine-discharge-pipe-effluent-standards.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-environment/O0040013-marine-discharge-pipe-effluent-standards.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These Standards are determined pursuant to Article 7, Paragraph 2 of the Water Pollution Control Act.

## Article 2

These Standards are applicable to enterprises or sewage systems discharging wastewater or sewage into the sea via marine discharge pipes.

## Article 3

Enterprises or sewage systems employing marine discharge pipes to discharge wastewater or sewage into the sea must comply with the limits specified in attached Table 1 and 2.

## Article 4

Concerning the limits in the foregoing paragraph, apart from the pH, which is defined as a range and not in units, the rest are defined as the maximum allowed value using the following units of measure:

I. E. Coli: colony count on filter membrane per 100 mL water sample (CFU/100mL).

II. Water temperature: Celsius (℃).

III. Other items: milligrams per liter (mg/L)

## Article 5

Unless an enforcement date is separately designated, these standards shall take effect on the date of promulgation.
