# Standard on Categories of Observation Instruments of Exclusive Observation Stations Obligatory for Calibration

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-transportation-and-communications/K0100007-standard-on-categories-of-observation-instruments-of-exclusive-observation.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-transportation-and-communications/K0100007-standard-on-categories-of-observation-instruments-of-exclusive-observation.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

This Standard is enacted in accordance with the terms in the fifth paragraph of Article 20, Meteorological Act.

## Article 2

The categories of observation instruments of exclusive observation stations obligatory for calibration are as follows:

1.air pressure;

2.temperature;

3.humidity;

4.precipitation;

5.wind direction;

6.wind speed;

7.sunshine duration; and

8.solar radiation.

## Article 3

This Standard shall become effective on the date of promulgation.
