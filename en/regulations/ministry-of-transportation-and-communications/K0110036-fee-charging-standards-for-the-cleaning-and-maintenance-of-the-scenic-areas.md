# Fee-charging Standards for the Cleaning and Maintenance of the Scenic Areas Managed by East Coast National Scenic Area Headquarters, Tourism Administration, Ministry of Transportation and Communications

## Article 1

These Standards are enacted in accordance with Subparagraph 2, Paragraph 1, Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

The scenic spots within the jurisdiction of this Headquarters for which entry fees are charged, and the fee standards, are shown in the attached chart.

## Article 3

These Standards shall take effect on the date of promulgation.
