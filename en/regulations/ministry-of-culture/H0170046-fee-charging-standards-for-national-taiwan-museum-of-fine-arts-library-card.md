# Fee-charging Standards for National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts Library Card Applications

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-culture/H0170046-fee-charging-standards-for-national-taiwan-museum-of-fine-arts-library-card.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-culture/H0170046-fee-charging-standards-for-national-taiwan-museum-of-fine-arts-library-card.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Standards are decreed in accordance with the Charges and Fees Act stated in Article 10,Paragraph 1.

## Article 2

An application fee of NT$50 would be charged by National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts Art Library.

## Article 3

The Standards are executed since the date of promulgation.
