# Fee-charging Standards for Professional Certification of Records Manager

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/national-development-council/A0030353-fee-charging-standards-for-professional-certification-of-records-manager.md](../../../zh/regulations/national-development-council/A0030353-fee-charging-standards-for-professional-certification-of-records-manager.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The standards are prescribed by paragraph 1 of the Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

Each examination participant must pay a registration fee of NT$500.

## Article 3

The registration fee of Article 2 shall be reviewed at least once every three years considering the fluctuation of expenses, costs, consumer price index, and other factors.

## Article 4

The standards shall come into force from the date of promulgation.

## History

1.According to the mandate at Tang-Chi No.1120012446B issued by NAA the standards was made and announced on December 27, 2023；shall come into force from the date of promulgation.
