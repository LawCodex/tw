# Fee-charging Standards for Application of Launch Vehicles Launch Permit

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/national-science-and-technology-council/H0160092-fee-charging-standards-for-application-of-launch-vehicles-launch-permit.md](../../../zh/regulations/national-science-and-technology-council/H0160092-fee-charging-standards-for-application-of-launch-vehicles-launch-permit.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These Standards are established in accordance with Article 10, Paragraph 1 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

Applicants for the launch permit of launch vehicles in accordance with Article 11, Paragraph 1 of the Space Development Act shall pay a review fee. The review fee for each application is NT$23,000.

## Article 3

Receipts shall be issued when fees are collected in accordance with these Standards.

## Article 4

After the payment of the review fees stipulated in these Standards, no refunds or retention shall be requested for any reason,except in cases where the applicant is eligible for a refund under the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 5

If a launch permit is withdrawn or revoked after issuance in accordance with Article 15 of the Regulations for Launch Permit for Launch Vehicle and Handling of Space Accidents, the paid review fee will not be refunded.

## Article 6

These Standards shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
