# Regulations on the Methods of Broadcasting Advertisements and the Distribution and Number of Advertisements of Radio and Television Businesses

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/national-communications-commission/P0050057-regulations-on-the-methods-of-broadcasting-advertisements-and-the-distribution.md](../../../zh/regulations/national-communications-commission/P0050057-regulations-on-the-methods-of-broadcasting-advertisements-and-the-distribution.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These regulations are enacted in accordance with Paragraph 4, Article 31 of the Cable Radio and Television Act.

## Article 2

Advertisements may be broadcast three times in programs that air for up to 30 minutes, and six times in programs that air for up to 60 minutes. For programs that air for more than 60 minutes, the number of advertisements that may be broadcast shall be determined using the aforementioned principle.

For live events, advertisements may be broadcast at appropriate times.

“Advertisements” as referred to in Paragraph 1 include those that are broadcast before or after programs as well as those broadcast during programs.

## Article 3

These regulations shall take effect from the date of promulgation.
