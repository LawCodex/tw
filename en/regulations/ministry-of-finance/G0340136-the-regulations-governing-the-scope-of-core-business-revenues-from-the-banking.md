# The Regulations Governing the Scope of Core Business revenues from the Banking and Insurance Business of the Banking and Insurance Industries

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-finance/G0340136-the-regulations-governing-the-scope-of-core-business-revenues-from-the-banking.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-finance/G0340136-the-regulations-governing-the-scope-of-core-business-revenues-from-the-banking.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Regulations herein are set forth pursuant to Paragraph 2, Article 11 of the Value-Added and Non-Value-Added Business Tax Act.

## Article 2

Core business revenues from the banking and insurance business of the banking and insurance industries shall be revenues separated from those listed below:

1.The non-core business revenues prescribed under Article 3 of the Regulations Governing the Scope of Non-Core Business Income of Banking, Insurance, Trust Investment, Securities, Futures, Commercial paper, and Pawn-Broking Businesses.

2.The revenues from underwriting, dealing, commission agency, brokerage, agency of securities prescribed under Article 15 of the Securities and Exchange Act.

3.The revenues from futures trading prescribed under Article 3 of the Futures Trading Act.

4.The revenues from certifying, underwriting, brokering and trading of short-term bills or financial bonds prescribed under Article 21 of the Act Governing Bills Finance Business.

5.The revenues from trust of money, trust of loans and related security interests, trust of securities prescribed under Article 16 of the Trust Enterprise Act.

6.The revenues from agency fee, commission or processing fee from insurance agents who negotiate an insurance contract or who provide related services as prescribed under Articles 8 and 9 of the Insurance Act.

7.The revenues from investing in short-term bills prescribed under Subparagraph 7 of Article 71 and Article 74-1 of The Banking Act of The Republic of China.

8.The revenues from dealing and holding certificates of deposits issued by the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) in accordance with Article 27 of The Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) Act.

During the implementation period of Subparagraph 7 of the preceding Paragraph, the same shall be applicable to the cases where the taxable tax is not yet levied or pending final decisions.

## Article 3

These Regulations shall take force on the day of promulgation, except that Subparagraph 7 of Paragraph 1 and Paragraph 2 of the preceding Article shall take force on January 1, 2022.
