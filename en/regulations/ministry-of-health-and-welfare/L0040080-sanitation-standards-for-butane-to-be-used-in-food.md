# Sanitation Standards for Butane to be Used in Food

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040080-sanitation-standards-for-butane-to-be-used-in-food.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040080-sanitation-standards-for-butane-to-be-used-in-food.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Standards are prescribed in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of the Act Governing Food Sanitation.

## Article 2

Butane as referred to these standards including both n-butane (CAS Reg. No. 106-97-8) and isobutane (CAS Reg. No. 75-28-5). The formula of the chemical is indicated as C4H10, and its molecular weight is 58.12.
