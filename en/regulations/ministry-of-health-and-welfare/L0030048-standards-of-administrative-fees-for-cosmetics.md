# Standards of Administrative Fees for Cosmetics

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0030048-standards-of-administrative-fees-for-cosmetics.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0030048-standards-of-administrative-fees-for-cosmetics.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These Standards are adopted pursuant to the provisions of Article 30 of the Cosmetic Hygiene and Safety Act (the Act) and Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

The standards for charging review fees, certificate fees, and inspection fees for cosmetics notification and applications for inspection of compliance with the Cosmetics Good Manufacturing Practice Regulations are as follows:

\1.	Cosmetics notification:

(1)	The review fee charged for cosmetics notification shall be NT$800 per application.

(2)	The review fee charged for amendments to a cosmetics notification shall be NT$700 per application.

(3)	The review fee charged for amendments to multiple cosmetics notifications shall be NT$700 for up to 30 notifications; an additional NT$100 shall be charged for each additional 30 notifications; any number of notifications fewer than 30 shall be counted as 30.

(4)	The review fee charged for extension of cosmetics notification shall be NT$600 per application.

\2.	Applications for animal testing for the safety assessment of cosmetics or cosmetics ingredients:

(1)	The review fee charged for animal testing for the safety assessment of cosmetics or cosmetics ingredients shall be NT$40,000 per application.

(2)	The review fee charged for amendments to an approved case shall be NT$10,000 per application.

(3)	For reissuance or replacement of approval document for animal testing for the safety assessment of cosmetics or cosmetics ingredients, NT$4,000 shall be charged per application.

\3.	The review fee charged for inquiry regarding the classification of cosmetic products shall be NT$3,500 per application.

\4.	Applications for inspection of compliance with the Cosmetics Good Manufacturing Practice Regulations:

(1)	For inspections or subsequent inspections of establishment, relocation, or expansion of manufacturing facilities for domestic cosmetics, new forms of cosmetics, or processed items, NT$60,000 shall be charged per application.

(2)	For on-site inspections or subsequent inspections of manufacturing facilities for foreign cosmetics, NT$600,000 shall be charged per application, including documents review of NT$60,000 and on-site inspection of NT$540,000.

(3)	For amendments to approval documents of cosmetics Good Manufacturing Practice, NT$6,000 shall be charged per application.

\5.	Cosmetics certificates and approval letters:

(1)	The document review fee charged for cosmetics manufacturing and sale certificates, imported cosmetics sales certificates, and cosmetics manufacturing certificates shall be NT$2,800 per application.

(2)	For Chinese cosmetics manufacturing and sale certificates, imported cosmetics sales certificates or cosmetics manufacturing certificates, NT$1,800 shall be charged for an original, and NT$200 shall be charged for a copy.

(3)	For English cosmetics manufacturing and sale certificates, imported cosmetics sales certificates or cosmetics manufacturing certificates, NT$1,800 shall be charged for an original, and NT$200 shall be charged for a copy.

(4)	NT$1,500 shall be charged for a Chinese cosmetics GMP compliance certificate.

(5)	NT$1,500 shall be charged for an English cosmetics GMP compliance certificate.

(6)	For reissuing lost approved documents of cosmetics GMP compliance, NT$1,800 shall be charged per application.

\6.	Applications for the approval of human cell-derived exosomes for use in cosmetics:

(1)	The review fee charged for human cell-derived exosomes for use in cosmetics shall be NT$100,000 per application.

(2)	The review fee charged for amendments to an approved case shall be NT$10,000 per application.

(3)	For reissuance or replacement of approval document for human cell-derived exosomes for use in cosmetics, NT$4,000 shall be charged per application.

Applications under Subparagraph 5, Items (1) through (3) of the preceding paragraph shall be limited to ten products per application.

## Article 3

The on-site inspection fee for auditors and experts set forth in Item (2), Subparagraph 4 of the preceding article shall apply mutatis mutandis to the Directions for the Overseas Travel Allowance Disbursement, and shall be charged to the audited entity by the central competent authority.

## Article 4

These standards shall be implemented from 1st, July 2026.
