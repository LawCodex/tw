# Sanitation Standards for Contaminants and Toxins in Foods

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040138-sanitation-standards-for-contaminants-and-toxins-in-foods.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040138-sanitation-standards-for-contaminants-and-toxins-in-foods.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Standards are prescribed in accordance with the provision of Article 17 of the Act Governing Food Safety and Sanitation.

## Article 2

Contaminants as referred to these standards shall mean the substance not intentionally added to food, which present in food as a result of production or pollution during the manufacturing, processing, preparing, packaging, transportation, storage, or selling, or as a result of environmental contamination, but do not include insect fragments, hairs and other extraneous foreign matters.

Toxins as referred to these standards including the mycotoxins, marine bio-toxins and natural toxins in plant origin.

The scope of these standards does not include the residues of pesticides, veterinary drugs and processing aids; the tolerance of atomic dust and radioactivity contamination, polychlorinated biphenyl and dioxins; and the microorganisms and microbial toxins that are within the Sanitation Standards for Microorganisms in Foods.

## Article 3

The maximum levels of metals in foods shall meet the standards in Annex table 1.

## Article 4

The maximum levels of mycotoxins in foods shall meet the standards in Annex table 2.

## Article 5

The maximum levels of other contaminants and toxins in foods shall meet the standards in Annex table 3.

## Article 6

The Standards shall be implemented on January 1, 2019.

The promulgation of amended Appendix 1 to Article 3, Appendix 2 to Article 4, Appendix 3 to Article 5 on June 17, 2020 shall be implemented on January 1, 2021.

The promulgation of amended Appendix 3 to Article 5 on February 4, 2021 shall be implemented on July 1, 2021.

The promulgation of amended Appendix 3 to Article 5 on May 31, 2022 shall be implemented on July 1, 2024.

The promulgation of amended Appendix 1 to Article 3 on March 28, 2024 shall be implemented on July 1, 2024.

The promulgation of amended Appendix 1 to Article 3 on November 28, 2024 shall be implemented on January 1, 2026.
