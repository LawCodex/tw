# Sanitation Standards for Health Food

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040145-sanitation-standards-for-health-food.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040145-sanitation-standards-for-health-food.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Standards are prescribed in accordance with the provisions of Article 11 of the Health Food Control Act.

## Article 2

The sanitation standards of health food as well as its containers and packaging shall be governed with the relevant provisions of the Act Governing Food Safety and Sanitation.

## Article 3

The Standards shall come into effect on the date of promulgation.
