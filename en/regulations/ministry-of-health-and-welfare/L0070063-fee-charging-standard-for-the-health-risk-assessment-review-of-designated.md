# Fee-charging Standard for the Health Risk Assessment Review of Designated Tobacco Products

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0070063-fee-charging-standard-for-the-health-risk-assessment-review-of-designated.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0070063-fee-charging-standard-for-the-health-risk-assessment-review-of-designated.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

This standard is enacted in accordance with Article 10 of the Charges And Fees Act.

## Article 2

Business operators applying for health risk assessments reviews of designated tobacco products shall pay a fee of NTD 2 million for each application .

## Article 3

The standard shall be enforced on the date of promulgation.
