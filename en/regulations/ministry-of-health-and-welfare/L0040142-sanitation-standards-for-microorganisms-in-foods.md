# Sanitation Standards for Microorganisms in Foods

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040142-sanitation-standards-for-microorganisms-in-foods.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040142-sanitation-standards-for-microorganisms-in-foods.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The standards are prescribed in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of the Act Governing Food Safety and Sanitation (hereinafter referred to as "this Act").

## Article 2

The scope of these standards encompasses microorganisms and their toxins or metabolites in food, excluding fungi and mycotoxins.

## Article 3

The limits for microorganisms and their toxins or metabolites in food shall comply with the requirements specified in the Annex table.

## Article 4

Food is found to be under any of the circumstances described in Subparagraph 4 of Paragraph 1 of Article 15 of this Act and Article 6 of the Rules for the Implementation of this Act, shall be subject to the provision of Paragraph 1 of Article 15 of this Act.

## Article 5

The Standards shall be implemented on July 1, 2021.
