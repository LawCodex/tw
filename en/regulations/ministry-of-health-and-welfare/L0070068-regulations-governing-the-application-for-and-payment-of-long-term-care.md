# Regulations Governing the Application for and Payment of Long-term Care Services for Foreign Professionals and Their Dependents

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0070068-regulations-governing-the-application-for-and-payment-of-long-term-care.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0070068-regulations-governing-the-application-for-and-payment-of-long-term-care.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These Regulations have been established pursuant to Article 29, Paragraph 4 of the Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”).

## Article 2

Pursuant to Article 29, Paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Act, foreign professionals, foreign specialist professionals, and foreign senior professionals, as well as their spouses, minor children, and adult children who lack self-care ability due to physical or mental disability (collectively referred to as “dependents,” excluding the primary applicant), may apply to a long-term care management center or the municipal or county (city) competent authority (hereinafter referred to as “local competent authority”) for long-term care services, provided they suffer from a physical or mental disability and meet one of the following qualifications:

\1. Aged 65 or over.

\2. Have been evaluated and assessed in accordance with Articles 6 and 7 of the People with Disabilities Rights Protection Act by a committee of professionals set forth in Article 5 of that Act, with the results complying with the same Act.

Applicants under the preceding paragraph must have been granted permanent residency by the National Immigration Agency of the Ministry of the Interior, and they must have lawfully resided in the Republic of China for ten cumulative years, with 183 days or more of residence each year.

## Article 3

When submitting an application under Paragraph 1 of the preceding Article, the applicant shall provide the following documents:

\1. An Alien Permanent Resident Certificate.

\2. Documentary proof establishing the dependent relationship.

\3. Documentary proof of compliance with the requirements of Paragraph 1, Subparagraph 2 of the preceding Article.

The qualifications specified in Paragraph 2 of the preceding Article shall be verified by the long-term care management center or local competent authority against residency information provided by the National Immigration Agency of the Ministry of the Interior pursuant to Article 29, Paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Act.

## Article 4

Upon assessment pursuant to Articles 8 and 8-1 of the Long-Term Care Services Act and determination that the applicant meets the eligibility criteria for long-term care service benefits, the local competent authority shall determine, based on the degree of disability, the applicant’s long-term care service level, the long-term care service benefit amount to be provided, and the proportion of expenses to be borne by the applicant. Services shall then be provided mutatis mutandis in accordance with the Regulations Governing the Application for and Payment of Long-term Care Services.

## Article 5

For an applicant who has been approved to receive long-term care services pursuant to the preceding Article, in accordance with Article 29, Paragraph 3 of the Act, if their permanent residency permit or qualification under Article 2, Paragraph 1, Subparagraph 2 of this Act is withdrawn, revoked, or otherwise invalidated, the local competent authority shall revoke the approval for receiving long-term care services and terminate the provision of services accordingly. However, this shall not apply to applications whose permanent residency permit is withdrawn or revoked due to the restoration, acquisition, or concurrent possession of Republic of China nationality.

## Article 6

These Regulations shall come into effect on June 30, 2026.
