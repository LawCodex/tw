# Fee-charging Standards for Examination and Certificate of Health Food Registration

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040127-fee-charging-standards-for-examination-and-certificate-of-health-food.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040127-fee-charging-standards-for-examination-and-certificate-of-health-food.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These standards are enacted in accordance with the provisions the Paragraph 2 of Article 7 of the Health Food Control Act and Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

The standards for charging fees to register health food are as follows:

1.The preliminary review fee shall be NT$ 160,000 per application.

2.The secondary review fee shall be NT$ 340,000 per application.

3.The review fee charged for amendment or extension of the registration certificate shall be NT$ 12,000 per application.

4.The review fee charged for reissuance, replacement or transference of the registration certificate shall be NT$ 16,000 per application.

5.The fee charged for a certificate shall be NT$ 2,000 each.

6.The fee charged for an English Certificate shall be NT$ 2,000 each for original copy and NT$ 100 each for duplicate.

Each application for amendment of the registration certificate in subparagraph 3 of the preceding paragraph shall be the different contents in the same registration certificate or the same item with the same contents in different ones.

## Article 3

These standards shall be implemented from January 1, 2025.
