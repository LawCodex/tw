# Fee-charging Standards for Licensure Exam Application for Nurse Practitioners

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0020126-fee-charging-standards-for-licensure-exam-application-for-nurse-practitioners.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0020126-fee-charging-standards-for-licensure-exam-application-for-nurse-practitioners.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

This Standard is established in accordance with Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

This Standard is applicable to the following:

\1. Fees for applying for written and oral tests in accordance with the Regulations Governing Specialties and Examination of Nurse Practitioner;

\2. Fees incurred by investigation of certificate renewal applications submitted in accordance with the Regulations Governing Specialties and Examination of Nurse Practitioner.

## Article 3

The amount of fees charged for licensure exam application for nurse practitioners is as follows:

\1. NT$1,500 per person for each written test;

\2. NT$2,000 per person for each oral test;

\3. NT$500 per person for each nurse practitioner certificate renewal investigation.

## Article 4

This Standard shall come into effect on the date of promulgation.

## History

1.All the four articles were established and promulgated as per the Department of Health's Order Wei-Shu-Zhao-Zi No. 0952800889 dated June 29, 2006; the four articles came into effect on the date of promulgation.

2.The provisions of Article 2 were amended and promulgated as per the Department of Health's Order Wei-Shu-Zhao-Zi No. 0962802216 dated November 13, 2007.

3.The title and the provisions of Articles 2 and 3 were amended and promulgated as per the Ministry of Health and Welfare's Order Wei-Bu-Zhao-Zi No. 1101560212 dated May 12, 2021 (the previous title: Fee Standard for Applications for Examination of Nurse Practitioners Specializing in Internal Medicine and Surgery; the new title: Fee-charging Standards for Licensure Exam Application for Nurse Practitioners).
