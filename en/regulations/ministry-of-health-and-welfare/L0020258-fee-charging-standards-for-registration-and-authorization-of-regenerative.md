# Fee-charging Standards for Registration and Authorization of Regenerative Medicine Cell Operation and Cell Bank Establishment

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0020258-fee-charging-standards-for-registration-and-authorization-of-regenerative.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0020258-fee-charging-standards-for-registration-and-authorization-of-regenerative.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Standards are enacted pursuant to Article 7 and Paragraph 1 of Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

Medical care institutions or regenerative medicine biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies submitting applications pursuant to the " Regenerative Medicine Cell Operation Management Regulations" shall pay fees as follows:

\1. New applications for a cell operation approval or substantial relocation of the operation site: NT$120,000.

\2. Change to operation items or operational content:

(1) Addition of operation items or operational content: NT$120,000.

(2) Reduction of operation items or operational content: NT$10,000.

\3. Change to operational areas:

(1) Addition of manipulation rooms or change of designated use: NT$120,000.

(2) Reduction of manipulation rooms: NT$10,000.

\4. Extension of validity period: NT$120,000.

\5. Changes of the name or address of the institution or the site: NT$10,000.

## Article 3

Medical care institutions or regenerative medicine biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies submitting applications pursuant to the " Regenerative Medicine Cell Bank Establishment and Licensing Management Regulations" shall pay the fees prescribed as follows:

\1. New application for cell bank establishment permit or substantial relocation of the operation site: NT$120,000.

\2. Change to items preserved by the cell bank:

(1) Addition of preservation items: NT$120,000.

(2) Reduction of preservation items: NT$10,000.

\3. Change to preservation areas:

(1) Addition of areas: NT$120,000.

(2) Reduction of areas: NT$10,000.

\4. Extension of validity period: NT$120,000.

\5. Change of the name or address of the establisher or the site of the cell bank: NT$10,000.

\6. Change of the responsible person, medical director, or quality director of the establisher: NT$10,000.

## Article 4

The Standards shall take effect on the date of the enforcement of the Regenerative Medicine Act.
