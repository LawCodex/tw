# Standards for veterinary drug residue limits in foods

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040026-standards-for-veterinary-drug-residue-limits-in-foods.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040026-standards-for-veterinary-drug-residue-limits-in-foods.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

This Standard is prescribed in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 15 of the Act Governing Food Safety and Sanitation.

## Article 2

The analysis of veterinary drug residues referred in this Standard shall be marker residue, including the drug itself and its significant relevant metabolites.

## Article 3

The veterinary drug residue limits in foods shall meet the following Table. Veterinary drugs not listed in the Table shall not be detected. For those listed veterinary drugs not permitted to be used by Council of Agriculture, Executive Yuan in the Republic of China shall only apply to the imported products.

## Article 4

This Standard shall be implemented from the date of promulgation.
