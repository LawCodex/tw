# Standards for Foods Treated with Ionising Radiation

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040096-standards-for-foods-treated-with-ionising-radiation.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-health-and-welfare/L0040096-standards-for-foods-treated-with-ionising-radiation.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Standards are prescribed in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of the Act Governing Food Safety and Sanitation.

## Article 2

This standard shall apply to processing of food to achieve the purpose of inhibiting germination or sprouting and microorganisms through treated with ionising radiation.

## Article 3

Food may be treated by the following sources of ionising radiation and its maximum energy level:

\1. Gamma(γ) rays from radionuclides Co-60.

\2. X-rays generated from electron accelerators: at or below of 5 MeV.

\3. Electrons generated from electron accelerators: at or below of 10 MeV.

## Article 5

The Standards shall be implemented on the date of promulgation.
