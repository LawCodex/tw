# National Palace Museum Fee Standards for Viewing,Copying or Duplicating Library and Archival Materials

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/national-palace-museum/H0190009-national-palace-museum-fee-standards-for-viewing-copying-or-duplicating-library.md](../../../zh/regulations/national-palace-museum/H0190009-national-palace-museum-fee-standards-for-viewing-copying-or-duplicating-library.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The fee standards are enacted according to Paragraph 1 of Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

The fees for copying or duplicating materials at the library of the National Palace Museum (hereafter as NPM) are listed in the attached document.

## Article 3

When permitted to view, copy or duplicate archives of the NPM, a fee is charged in accordance with the Fee Standards to View, Copy or Duplicate Archives of the National Archives Administration.

## Article 4

The fee standards shall take effect from the date of promulgation.
