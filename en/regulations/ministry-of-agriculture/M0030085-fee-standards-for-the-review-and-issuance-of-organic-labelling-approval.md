# Fee Standards for the Review and Issuance of Organic Labelling Approval Documents for Organic Agricultural Products and Organic Processed Agricultural Products

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-agriculture/M0030085-fee-standards-for-the-review-and-issuance-of-organic-labelling-approval.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-agriculture/M0030085-fee-standards-for-the-review-and-issuance-of-organic-labelling-approval.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These Standards are established in accordance with Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

The fees for reviewing the applications made by importers for the issuance of organic labelling approval document are described below:

1.New Taiwan Dollars Five Hundred (NTD 500) for each application covering 1-5 products;

2.New Taiwan Dollars One Thousand (NTD 1,000) for each application covering 6-10 products;

3.New Taiwan Dollars One Thousand and Five Hundred (NTD 1,500) for each application covering 11-20 products; and

4.New Taiwan Dollars Two Thousand (NTD 2,000) for each application covering more than 20 products.

## Article 3

These Standards shall come into force on the date of promulgation.
