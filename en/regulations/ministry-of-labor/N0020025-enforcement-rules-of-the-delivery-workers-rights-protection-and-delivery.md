# Enforcement Rules of the Delivery Workers’ Rights Protection and Delivery Platform Management Act

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-labor/N0020025-enforcement-rules-of-the-delivery-workers-rights-protection-and-delivery.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-labor/N0020025-enforcement-rules-of-the-delivery-workers-rights-protection-and-delivery.md)) is authoritative.

## Chapter I General Provisions

### Article 1

The Enforcement Rules are enacted in accordance with Article 27 of the Delivery Workers' Rights Protection and Delivery Platform Management Act (hereinafter referred to as the “Act”).

### Article 2

The remuneration referred to in Subparagraph 4 of Article 3 of the Act does not include tips, goods in kind, or other payments that are not consideration for services, paid to a delivery worker by a consumer, partner merchant, or delivery platform operator.

### Article 3

The delivery service period referred to in Subparagraph 7 of Article 3 of the Act shall be calculated in minutes. Times of less than thirty seconds shall be disregarded; times of thirty seconds or more shall be counted as one minute.

## Chapter II Protection of Delivery Workers’ Rights and Interests

### Article 4

When calculating the minimum basic remuneration payable per order in accordance with Paragraph 1 of Article 5 of the Act, a delivery platform operator shall first calculate the amount to two decimal places and then round it up to one decimal place.

When adjusting and publicly announcing the guaranteed minimum amount of basic remuneration per order in accordance with Paragraph 2 of Article 5 of the Act, the central competent authority shall multiply the guaranteed amount for the preceding year by the annual rate of increase in the hourly minimum wage prescribed under the Minimum Wage Act, calculate the resulting amount to two decimal places, and then round it up to one decimal place.

### Article 5

A delivery platform operator may provide the delivery worker with the remuneration statement referred to in Paragraph 4 of Article 5 of the Act by hard copy, electronic transmission, or any other means that enables the delivery worker to retain and export the information.

### Article 6

Upon the death of a delivery worker, the delivery platform operator shall settle any outstanding remuneration and pay it to the delivery worker's heirs as prescribed under the Civil Code.

### Article 7

The estimated remuneration referred to in Article 6 of the Act shall include basic remuneration and bonuses, which shall be disclosed separately and shall not be disclosed solely as an aggregate amount.

### Article 8

The pickup and delivery place referred to in Article 6 of the Act shall include the address, floor, designated delivery area, or other information sufficient to clearly identify the delivery place.

The term “designated delivery area” referred to in the preceding paragraph means the specific area at which a delivery worker is required to complete delivery of the goods, including a building management office, conference room, office, parking lot, gatehouse, or other equivalent area.

A delivery platform operator shall establish appropriate fields in its designated application or device to enable consumers to accurately enter their designated delivery place. Where the delivery place information provided by a delivery platform operator to a delivery worker is inconsistent with the actual circumstances, the delivery platform operator shall, upon proving that the inconsistency was not attributable to its intent or negligence, be deemed to have clearly informed the delivery worker of the material information concerning the delivery place.

### Article 9

The other relevant material information referred to in Article 6 of the Act shall include at least the following:

I. The driving route and estimated travel distance calculated by a credible mapping application.

II. The estimated time for completion of the order.

III. The consumer's payment method.

IV. The contents and quantity of the goods.

### Article 10

A delivery platform operator terminating a delivery service contract pursuant to Paragraph 1 of Article 7 of the Act shall do so within thirty days from the date on which it becomes aware of the relevant circumstances or the resulting harm.

With respect to the reasons for terminating a delivery service contract or making any other adverse decision pursuant to Paragraph 2 of Article 7 of the Act, the delivery platform operator shall provide the delivery worker with a summary of the relevant facts and shall bear the burden of proof. Where such evidence involves the protection of a whistleblower or the privacy and safety of a relevant third party, information sufficient to identify such person may be redacted.

### Article 11

A delivery platform operator shall publicly disclose the following time limits for handling complaints in accordance with Paragraph 2 of Article 9 of the Act:

I. For complaints accepted in accordance with Paragraph 1 of Article 9 of the Act, the handling period shall commence on the day following the day of accepting the complaint and shall not exceed twenty-one days.

II. For complaints accepted in accordance with Paragraph 3 of Article 9 of the Act, the handling period shall commence on the day following the day of accepting the complaint and shall not exceed forty days.

### Article 12

A delivery platform operator may commission a non-profit civil organization to handle the operation and related matters of the independent review panel established in accordance with Paragraph 3 of Article 9 of the Act.

Where the independent review panel determines that a delivery worker's complaint is upheld, the delivery platform operator shall respect the determination of the independent review panel unless it has justifiable reasons not to do so.

A delivery platform operator shall publicly disclose a roster of the members of the independent review panel referred to in Paragraph 3 of Article 9 of the Act. The same shall apply whenever there is a change in members.

### Article 13

The phrase “no record of providing delivery services for more than three consecutive days” referred to in Paragraph 2 of Article 10 of the Act shall mean that, commencing at 0:00 on the day following the delivery worker's completion of the last order of the day, the delivery worker does not complete any further order for three consecutive days. However, any period during which a delivery platform operator suspends operations in accordance with Article 15 of the Act shall not be included.

### Article 14

A delivery worker applying to a delivery platform operator in accordance with Article 12 of the Act for payment of the portion of Labor Occupational Accident Insurance premiums borne by the delivery worker shall comply with the following requirements:

I. Eligibility: Where a delivery worker has paid Labor Occupational Accident Insurance premiums and provided delivery services during the month concerned, the delivery worker may apply to the delivery platform operator for payment of the portion of insurance premiums borne by the delivery worker for that month.

II. Proof of premium payment: A receipt issued by an occupational labor union pursuant to Paragraph 2 of Article 36 of the Enforcement Rules of the Labor Occupational Accident Insurance and Protection Act, or proof of premium payment pursuant to Article 10 of the Regulations Governing Enrollment into the Insurance in Accordance with Article 10 of Labor Occupational Accident Insurance and Protection Act.

III. Application period: Within one year commencing from the first day of the month following the month in which the delivery worker provided delivery services.

The delivery platform operator shall pay the insurance premiums referred to in the preceding paragraph within thirty days from the date on which the delivery worker submits the application.

## Chapter III Delivery Platform Management

### Article 15

A delivery platform operator under any of the circumstances prescribed in Article 15 of the Act providing any order to a delivery worker in an area where operations have not been suspended shall not specify a driving route for pickup or delivery of goods that passes through an area where operations have been suspended.

### Article 16

The relevant authorities referred to in Paragraph 2 of Article 18, Paragraph 2 of Article 19, and Paragraph 2 of Article 20 of the Act are the competent authorities and the competent authorities of the respective industries referred to in Article 2 of the Act.

### Article 17

The requirement under Article 21 of the Act to notify the local labor inspection authority within eight hours means that a delivery platform operator shall, within eight hours from the time it knows or should have known that a prescribed occupational accident has occurred, notify the labor inspection authority having jurisdiction over the place where the occupational accident occurred.

## Chapter IV Supplementary Provisions

### Article 18

The authorities responsible for imposing the administrative fines referred to in Paragraph 5 of Article 24 of the Act are as follows:

I. Administrative fines for a delivery platform operator's violation of Paragraph 5 of Article 4; Paragraphs 1 and 3 through 5 of Article 5; Paragraph 3 of Article 8; Article 9; Article 10; Paragraph 1 of Article 12; or Paragraph 1 of Article 20 of the Act shall be imposed by the special municipality or county (city) competent authority.

II. Administrative fines for a delivery platform operator's violation of Paragraph 3 of Article 14 or Paragraph 1 of Article 19 of the Act shall be imposed by the competent authority of economic affairs.

III. Administrative fines for a delivery platform operator's violation of Paragraph 1 of Article 18 or Paragraphs 1 or 2 of Article 22 concerning traffic safety education and training shall be imposed by the competent authority of transportation.

IV. Administrative fines for a delivery platform operator's violation of Paragraphs 1 or 2 of Article 22 concerning food safety and hygiene education and training shall be imposed by the competent authority of health and welfare.

### Article 19

The competent authorities and competent authorities of the respective industries may entrust their subordinate agencies or commission the special municipality or county (city) competent authorities to handle matters prescribed in Chapter II, Protection of Delivery Workers' Rights and Interests; Chapter III, Delivery Platform Management; and Chapter IV, Penalty, of the Act.

### Article 20

These Enforcement Rules shall enter into force on July 21, 2026.
