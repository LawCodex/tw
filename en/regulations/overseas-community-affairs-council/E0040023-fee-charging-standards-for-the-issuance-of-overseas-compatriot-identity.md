# Fee-Charging Standards for the Issuance of Overseas Compatriot Identity Certificates by the Overseas Community Affairs Council, R.O.C.（TAIWAN）

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/overseas-community-affairs-council/E0040023-fee-charging-standards-for-the-issuance-of-overseas-compatriot-identity.md](../../../zh/regulations/overseas-community-affairs-council/E0040023-fee-charging-standards-for-the-issuance-of-overseas-compatriot-identity.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These Standards are established in accordance with Article 14 of the Overseas Compatriot Identity Certification Act.

## Article 2

The Overseas Community Affairs Council, R.O.C.（TAIWAN） shall charge a fee of NT$250 for each Overseas Compatriot Identity Certificate issued upon application.

## Article 3

These Standards shall enter into force on July 1, 2026.
