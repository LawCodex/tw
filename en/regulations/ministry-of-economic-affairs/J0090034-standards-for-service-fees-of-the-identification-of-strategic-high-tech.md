# Standards for Service Fees of the Identification of Strategic High-Tech Commodities, Ministry of Economic Affairs

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-economic-affairs/J0090034-standards-for-service-fees-of-the-identification-of-strategic-high-tech.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-economic-affairs/J0090034-standards-for-service-fees-of-the-identification-of-strategic-high-tech.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These standards are enacted pursuant to Paragraph 1 of Article 10 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

All fees for services offered by the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) for identifying whether exported or imported goods are strategic high-tech commodities shall be governed by these standards.

## Article 3

The fee for applying for the identification of goods is NT$2,000 per case.

The identifications applicable to these standards are based on document identification, with the exception of on-site identification.

When on-site identification is deemed necessary, the applicant shall pay for the transportation cost, miscellaneous fees, and accommodation expenses in accordance with instructions concerning the Directions for Domestic Travel Allowance Disbursement.

## Article 4

These standards shall become effective on January 1, 2024.
