# Regulations Governing Mobile Broadband Dedicated Telecommunications Network Fee-Charging Standards

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-digital-affairs/K0060163-regulations-governing-mobile-broadband-dedicated-telecommunications-network-fee.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-digital-affairs/K0060163-regulations-governing-mobile-broadband-dedicated-telecommunications-network-fee.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Telecommunications Management Act, Article 92.

## Article 2

For the review, inspection and license issuance, renewal, and reissuance of the establishment of mobile broadband dedicated telecommunications networks, the fee-charging standards are as per the attached table.

## Article 3

After paying the fees in accordance with the fee-charging standards, the applicant shall not apply for a refund unless the fee is overpaid or paid in error, or otherwise provided by laws and regulations.

## Article 4

The Regulations shall be enforced as of the date of promulgation.
