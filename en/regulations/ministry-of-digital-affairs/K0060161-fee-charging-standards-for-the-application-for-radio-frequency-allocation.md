# Fee-charging Standards for the Application for Radio Frequency Allocation Review by Telecommunications Enterprises

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-digital-affairs/K0060161-fee-charging-standards-for-the-application-for-radio-frequency-allocation.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-digital-affairs/K0060161-fee-charging-standards-for-the-application-for-radio-frequency-allocation.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These Standards are promulgated according to Article 92 of the Telecommunications Management Act (hereinafter referred to as the Act).

## Article 2

With regard to applications for radio frequency allocation review by telecommunications enterprises according to Paragraph 1, Article 53 of the Act, the review fees are as follows:

\1. Mobile communications frequency allocation: NT$1 million per piece.

\2. Satellite communications frequency allocation:

(1) For newly established satellite systems: NT$1 million per piece. The same shall apply to applications for both fixed-satellite and mobile-satellite services under the same satellite system.

(2) For existing satellite systems applying for additional frequency purposes: NT$1 million per piece.

(3) For existing satellite systems applying for additional or modified frequencies or installation sites without adding new frequency purposes: NT$200,000 per piece. If additional or modified frequency bands are involved, NT$500,000 per piece.

(4) Application for allocation of originally assigned frequencies upon expiry of the frequency use period: NT$5,000 per piece.

## Article 3

Applicants who have paid the fees as stipulated in these Standards may not request a refund, except in the case of overpayment, payment by mistake or as otherwise provided for in the regulations.

## Article 4

These Standards shall come into effect on the date of promulgation.
