# Standards for Funeral Director Certificate Fees

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-the-interior/D0020075-standards-for-funeral-director-certificate-fees.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-the-interior/D0020075-standards-for-funeral-director-certificate-fees.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Standards for Funeral director Certificate Fees (hereinafter referred to as the Standards) are formulated in accordance with Article 7 and Paragraph 1, Article 10 of the  Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

The funeral director certificate fees are charged as follows:

\1. According to Articles 3 and 8 of the Regulations for Administration on Funeral Director, the funeral director certificate fee is NT$1,000 per issuance or renewal.

\2. According to Article 10 of the Regulations for Administration on Funeral Director, the funeral director certificate fee is NT$600 per re-issuance.

## Article 3

The Standards shall be implemented from the date of promulgation.
