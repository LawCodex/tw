# Fee-charging Standards for Issuance of Police Criminal Record Certificates

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-the-interior/D0080159-fee-charging-standards-for-issuance-of-police-criminal-record-certificates.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-the-interior/D0080159-fee-charging-standards-for-issuance-of-police-criminal-record-certificates.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

The Standards are enacted in accordance with Article 9 of the Act Governing Issuance of Police Criminal Record Certificates and Paragraph 1, Article 10 of the Charges And Fees Act.

## Article 2

Each application for issuance of the police criminal record certificates shall be charged NT$100 per copy, and NT$20 for each additional copy.

## Article 3

The Standards will take effect from the date of promulgation.
