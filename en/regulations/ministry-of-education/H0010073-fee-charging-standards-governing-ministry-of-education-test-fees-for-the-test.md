# Fee-charging Standards Governing Ministry of Education Test Fees for the Test of Chinese as a Foreign Language

> Translation. The Chinese text ([zh/regulations/ministry-of-education/H0010073-fee-charging-standards-governing-ministry-of-education-test-fees-for-the-test.md](../../../zh/regulations/ministry-of-education/H0010073-fee-charging-standards-governing-ministry-of-education-test-fees-for-the-test.md)) is authoritative.

## Article 1

These Standards have been formulated in accordance with the provisions of Article 10, Paragraph 1 of the Charges and Fees Act.

## Article 2

To promote Taiwan’s Chinese language education, encourage foreign nationals around the world to learn Chinese, and establish Taiwan’s complete, standardized system for Chinese language teaching, the Ministry of Education has commissioned the Steering Committee for the Test of Proficiency–Huayu to conduct the Test of Chinese as a Foreign Language.

People taking a Test of Chinese as a Foreign Language test at any test level shall pay a registration fee in accordance with the following provisions:

1.Listening and reading test: NTD 2,000.

2.Speaking test: NTD 1,400.

3.Writing test: NTD 1,400.

4.Children's Chinese Competency Certification test: NTD 800.

An application for a rescore of a test in any category is subject to a review fee of NTD 250 for each level.

An application for the reissue or an additional copy of a score report for any category of test, is subject to a processing fee of NTD 250 per copy; an application for the reissue or an additional copy of a certificate is subject to a processing fee of NTD 350 per copy.

## Article 3

The fees specified in these Standards shall be reviewed at least once every three years, taking into account the costs to conduct the tests, cost variation trends, and changes in the consumer price index, which are factors affecting costs.

## Article 4

These Standards shall take effect on the date of promulgation.
